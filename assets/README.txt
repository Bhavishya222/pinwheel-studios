PINWHEEL STUDIOS WEBSITE (v2) — QUICK EDIT CHEAT SHEET
======================================================

1. PREVIEW ON YOUR PC:
   - Unzip this folder anywhere on your computer.
   - Double-click "index.html" to open it in your browser.
   - Click "Documentation" in the top bar to open "docs.html".

2. WHEN YOUR UNITY ASSET STORE PAGE GOES LIVE:
   - Open "index.html" and search for:
       Unity Asset Store — Coming Soon
   - Replace that <span> with:
       <a href="YOUR_ASSET_STORE_URL" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="btn btn-gold">View on Unity Asset Store ↗</a>

3. WHEN "THOU SHALT NOT PASS" STEAM PAGE IS APPROVED:
   - Open "index.html" and search for:
       Steam Page — Coming Soon
   - Replace that <span> with:
       <a href="YOUR_STEAM_URL" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="btn btn-primary">View on Steam ↗</a>
